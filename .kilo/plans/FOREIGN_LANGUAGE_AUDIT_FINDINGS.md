## ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS

1. **OCR Degradation:** Source PDF OCR produced "Pan-Xlaven" (Pan-Slav), "Pan-Duc" (Pan-Deutsch), "Hit-lê", "Sta-lin", garbled footnote references like "tr. ²⁹260 tt."

2. **Paragraph Segmentation:** Running headers ("CHỦ NGHĨA ĐẾ QUỐC", "TƯ DUY VỀ CHỦNG TỘC TRƯỚC...", etc.) were incorrectly prepended to paragraph text instead of being treated as separate heading elements.

3. **Chapter Title Merging:** Chapter titles were merged with the first body paragraph instead of being separate heading elements.

4. **Footnote Inline Content:** In ch12, footnote definitions were left inline in the body instead of being moved to the FOOTNOTES block.

5. **Empty Paragraphs:** ch02 P001-P026, ch01 P004-P005, ch10 P007-P008 are empty — likely lost during OCR or segmentation.

6. **Duplicate Paragraphs:** ch09 P008=P009, ch10 P003=P004, ch13 P009=P010 — likely from page-boundary duplication.

7. **Untranslated Foreign Text:** German, French, Latin, English passages left untranslated in body text.
